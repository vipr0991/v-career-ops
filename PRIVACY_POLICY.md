# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 15, 2026  

---

### 1. Overview & Nature of the Platform
Career-Ops ("we", "us", or "the Platform") is a private, personal-use web application designed exclusively to assist the owner in organizing, analyzing, and conducting their personal job search. 

**Career-Ops is strictly non-commercial:**
- It is **not** intended for commercial advertising, marketing campaigns, lead generation, selling products or services, or promotional activities.
- It is configured with strict search engine non-indexation directives (`noindex, nofollow, noarchive`) and is not intended for public indexing or discovery on search engines.

---

### 2. Purpose of Career-Ops
The platform exists solely to streamline personal career development and job search operations by:
1. **Curating High-Quality Opportunities:** Discovering relevant career openings across target markets (including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, and Bangalore, India) without the clutter of irrelevant, duplicate, or expired job postings.
2. **Tailoring Application Materials:** Customizing résumé versions (in LaTeX and ATS-optimized PDF formats) and strategic presentations for specific target opportunities.
3. **Evaluating Role & Company Credibility:** Analyzing company business models, growth trajectories, and job requirements to assess role fit before deciding to submit an application.

---

### 3. Information We Collect
Because Career-Ops is a personal career management utility, data collection is limited to operational inputs necessary for the tool's functionality:
- **Application Documents & Profile Data:** Résumés, CV files, work history, portfolio links, and contact details uploaded or configured by the user.
- **Job Search & Evaluation Inputs:** Job descriptions, job titles, target companies, notes, and geographic preferences entered or imported for analysis.
- **System & Activity Records:** Generated evaluation scores, customized document archives, exclusion lists, and temporary system operational logs.

---

### 4. How Information Is Used
Information processed by Career-Ops is used exclusively to:
- Score and match job descriptions against the user's qualifications and career goals.
- Generate tailored LaTeX source files, PDF résumé downloads, and strategic PowerPoint decks.
- Maintain a persistent exclusion registry so irrelevant or dismissed jobs are not repeatedly surfaced.
- Track application stages within the user's private pipeline command center.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & API Processing
To perform search and reasoning tasks requested by the user, Career-Ops interacts with selected third-party API providers:
- **Artificial Intelligence Engines:** (e.g., Google Gemini / Vertex AI) used strictly to evaluate job criteria, research company context, and tailor resume bullet points.
- **Job Aggregation Providers:** (e.g., JSearch, Adzuna, SerpAPI) used solely to query public job listings based on user-defined keywords and locations.

*We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user data, uploaded resumes, or job search activity with any third-party marketing network or data broker.*

---

### 6. Cookies, Tracking & Analytics
- Career-Ops **does not** utilize advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, third-party analytics trackers, or commercial marketing SDKs.
- Local browser storage (e.g., `localStorage`) is used strictly for essential user preferences, UI drawer states, and application session performance.

---

### 7. Data Retention & Deletion Controls
- All resume files, pipeline records, research notes, and exclusion memories remain under the direct control of the user.
- The user may delete uploaded files, clear evaluation reports, or purge excluded records directly through the platform interface at any time.

---

### 8. Data Security
We implement reasonable technical and architectural safeguards to protect stored data, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, strict environment-level API key management, and isolated application hosting environments.

---

### 9. Search Engine Non-Indexation (`noindex`)
Career-Ops enforces search engine exclusion directives via HTTP headers (`X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive`) and `robots.txt` configuration to prevent search engine crawlers from indexing site content or user materials.

---

### 10. User Rights & Data Management
As the sole operator and user of this personal platform, you maintain complete control over all data:
- **Right to Access & Export:** You may export or download all tailored resumes (PDF/LaTeX), decks, and evaluations at any time.
- **Right to Modify & Purge:** You may modify profile settings or permanently delete individual pipeline records, uploaded CVs, or exclusion entries.
